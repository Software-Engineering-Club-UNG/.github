# Software Engineering Club @ UNG

**The Software Engineering Club at the University of North Georgia, in Dahlonega, Georgia.** Putting UNG on the map.

We get UNG students onto teams for hackathons and programming contests, and we're building a home for computer science on campus. Membership is free and open to every UNG student, in any major. No coding experience needed.

**[codehawks.org](https://codehawks.org/)** · [Discord](https://discord.gg/RQ6xCMpRQm) · [UNG Connect](https://connect.ung.edu/organization/app-development-club-of-ung--dah-) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/swe-at-ung/)

## What we do

- **Hackathons.** We run [Hacklonega](https://hacklonega.dev/), UNG's first hackathon (October 24, 2026), and send teams to HackGT, Hacklanta and CodeLinc.
- **Competitive programming.** We practice together and send teams to ICPC.
- **Community.** Show your projects, get unstuck and find teammates.
